Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about TenderLink

What types of tenders does TenderLink provide?

TenderLink provides access to both government and private sector tenders across various industries including construction, IT services, healthcare, and more. Our platform consolidates tenders from multiple sources into one easy-to-use interface.

How does the eligibility assessment work?

Our eligibility assessment tool analyzes your business profile (size, certifications, past projects) and matches it with tender requirements. You'll receive a compatibility score and clear guidance on whether you qualify, along with recommendations for improving your eligibility if needed.

Can I collaborate with other businesses on tenders?

Yes! Our Professional and Enterprise plans include collaboration tools that allow you to connect with complementary businesses, form consortia for larger projects, and even co-author bid documents within our platform.

How often is tender information updated?

We update our database in real-time as new tenders are published and existing ones are modified. Our system continuously monitors hundreds of sources to ensure you never miss an opportunity or work with outdated information.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 14-day free trial for our Professional plan so you can experience the full power of TenderLink. No credit card required during the trial period.
