Why Driving Licence is Important?

Legal, safety and identification reasons to have a valid Driving Licence

Having a valid Driving Licence is not just a legal requirement but also an important document for identification and safety. Here's why you should always maintain a valid DL:

Legal Requirement

Driving without a valid licence can lead to heavy fines, vehicle impoundment or even imprisonment under the Motor Vehicles Act.

Valid ID Proof

Driving Licence is accepted as a valid proof of identity and address across India for various official purposes.

Insurance Coverage

Most vehicle insurance policies become void if the driver doesn't have a valid driving licence at the time of accident.

Road Safety

The DL process ensures drivers have minimum required knowledge of traffic rules and vehicle operation skills.
