Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

AIR Pratik HVAC Pvt. Ltd. ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following data about you:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Name, email address, telephone numbers, company name, and job title provided via our contact forms.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, time zone setting, and operating system.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, products, and services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, quote requests, and consultation bookings.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our HVAC engineering and maintenance services.
  • To send administrative information, updates, and technical notices.
  • To analyze website usage and optimize our digital presence.

3. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, accessed in an unauthorized way, altered, or disclosed. Access to your personal data is limited to those employees, agents, and contractors who have a business need to know.
