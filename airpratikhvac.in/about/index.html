About the company

End-to-end HVAC delivery with a strong service mindset

Airpratik HVAC is a Pune-based Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (HVAC) company established in 2011. We provide complete solutions starting from designing and consultation through supply, installation, testing, and maintenance.

We have executed HVAC installations for corporate offices, IT parks, institutions, hospitals, commercial buildings, residential buildings, multiplexes, shopping malls, telecommunication facilities, and many other projects across the country.

We believe in customer satisfaction, competent performance, and reliability. Every aspect of our work — sales, service, maintenance, and consulting — is focused on delivering value to the end user.

Airpratik HVAC also operates across integrated trading, contracting, and after-sales service, offering end-to-end solutions for customers with varied project requirements.

Our Business Segments

In accordance with the nature of products, markets, business drivers, and competitive positioning, the business is segmented as follows:

Electro-Mechanical Projects and Packaged Air Conditioning Systems

This segment covers the design, supply, installation, commissioning, and maintenance of central air conditioning plants, packaged and ducted systems, and VRF systems, along with electrification, plumbing, fire-fighting, revamp, retrofit, and upgrade services.

Professional Electronics and Industrial Systems

This segment includes sales and service of professional electronic equipment and industrial systems, with expertise in specialized markets such as testing, measurement, data communication, healthcare, and allied industrial applications.

Who we are

A legacy built on scope-first execution, continuous uptime, and lifecycle maintainability.

AIR Pratik HVAC Pvt. Ltd. is focused on executing HVAC projects and lifecycle services with enterprise-grade discipline. We operate with a clear objective: deliver stable, serviceable systems that align with real operating constraints.

Instead of treating HVAC as a one-time installation, we engineer outcomes across the full lifecycle—planning, execution, commissioning, and sustainment. This reduces post-handover instability and helps facilities maintain predictable uptime.

What we optimize for

Execution-ready planning that reduces rework and procurement churn
Quality gates during installation to prevent late-stage surprises
Handover documentation designed to support operations and audits
Maintenance strategy aligned to asset criticality and uptime expectations
