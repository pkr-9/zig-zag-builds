System Readout

Water-cooled centrifugal chillers are designed for massive cooling loads, utilizing cooling towers to achieve superior energy efficiency. They are the standard for mega-campuses, district cooling, and large hospitals, requiring strict water quality management and AMC discipline.

Key Capabilities
  • Industry-leading efficiency for massive loads
  • Long lifecycle with proper maintenance
  • Ideal for district cooling and mega-campuses

System Overview

Water-cooled centrifugal chillers are the backbone of large-scale central utility plants. By utilizing cooling towers for water-side heat rejection, they achieve unparalleled energy efficiency for massive cooling loads found in district cooling, massive IT campuses, and large healthcare facilities.

Best-Fit Applications

  • Mega-campuses
  • Large industrial facilities
