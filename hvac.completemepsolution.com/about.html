Technical strengths

Work approach

A HVAC-focused method for busy sites

Understand the use of the space

We begin with the room function, occupancy, operating hours, heat load sensitivity and project deadline.

Review routing and constraints

HVAC routes are checked with ceiling, electrical, plumbing, civil and interior work before execution moves ahead.

Execute with service access in mind

Filters, valves, drain points, panels and equipment access are considered so the system remains serviceable.

Support handover and upkeep

Testing, commissioning and AMC support help the system move from installation to reliable daily operation.
