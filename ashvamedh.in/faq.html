Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to common questions about Ashwamedh Group.

What is Ashwamedh Group?

Ashwamedh Group is structured as an integrated business ecosystem covering opportunity discovery, compliance, legal support, technology, finance, construction and interiors, and hiring. Services can be engaged independently or combined around a larger business requirement.

What services does Ashwamedh provide?

The current ecosystem includes TenderLink, CA Service, Legal Service, IT Service, Finance Service, Civil & Interior, and Hiring Agency. Each vertical has a defined scope and can support related requirements across the business lifecycle.

Can I use multiple Ashwamedh services for the same project?

Yes. The ecosystem is designed to support connected requirements. For example, a tender-led project can involve TenderLink for opportunity discovery, Finance for guarantees or working capital, Legal for contract review, and Civil & Interior or Hiring for execution.

Does Ashwamedh work with MSMEs and growing businesses?

The service model is designed to support MSMEs and growing organisations that may need specialist capabilities without building every function internally.

What does TenderLink help with?

TenderLink focuses on opportunity discovery, eligibility screening, bid or no-bid assessment, response preparation, deadline and clarification tracking, and connected support for documentation, finance and execution.
