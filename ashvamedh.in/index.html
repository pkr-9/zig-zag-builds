Questions businesses often ask

Clear answers to common questions about Ashwamedh Group and its business ecosystem.

What does Ashwamedh Group do?

Ashwamedh Group is an integrated business ecosystem with services spanning tender discovery, compliance, legal, technology, finance, civil and interior execution, and hiring.

Which businesses can use Ashwamedh services?

The service model is designed for MSMEs, contractors, project-led businesses, technology companies, manufacturers, service organisations and established enterprises that need one or more specialist capabilities.

What services does Ashwamedh Group provide?

Ashwamedh Group connects TenderLink, CA and compliance support, legal services, IT and software services, finance services, civil and interior execution, and hiring support.

Can Ashwamedh support a requirement involving multiple departments?

Yes. The ecosystem is structured so related service verticals can coordinate around a larger business requirement instead of treating every requirement as an isolated function.

How can a business start an enquiry with Ashwamedh Group?

A business can contact Ashwamedh Group with a requirement summary or supporting information through the contact page on the website.

Where does Ashwamedh Group provide services?

Ashwamedh Group's service model is positioned for businesses and organisations across India.
