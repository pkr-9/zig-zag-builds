CA Service — common questions

Direct answers about CA Service and the way it fits within the Ashwamedh Group business ecosystem.

What does the CA Service cover?

Ashwamedh's CA Service covers accounting, bookkeeping, audit, direct and indirect tax, company incorporation, ROC and secretarial compliance, and CFO-level financial advisory.

Who can use the CA Service?

The service is designed for founders, growing businesses and established organisations that need accounting, tax, audit, compliance or financial reporting support.

Can Ashwamedh manage recurring business compliance?

Yes. The service is structured around a recurring compliance cycle covering accounting, reconciliation, tax filings, audit preparation and statutory records.

Does the CA Service support new company incorporation?

Yes. The existing service scope includes company incorporation and the related setup of books, registrations and recurring statutory compliance workflows.

Does the CA Service include tax and audit support?

Yes. The service covers direct and indirect tax, GST, TDS, statutory and internal audit, reconciliation and related compliance documentation.

Can CA and Finance requirements be handled together?

Yes. CA and finance requirements can be coordinated where financial statements, CMA data, funding proposals, working capital or other financial documentation need to work together.

Can the CA Service support businesses preparing for tenders?

Yes. Financial statements, turnover or net-worth documentation and other financial records can support tender eligibility and bid requirements where applicable.

Our discipline

Compliance is not paperwork. It is financial credibility.

Banks, buyers, investors and procurement officers all read the same thing: your filings. Late returns, qualified audit reports and unreconciled books quietly disqualify you from opportunities you never hear about.

We run your statutory calendar as a managed function. Books closed on time, returns filed on schedule, and every number defensible when someone asks for evidence.

Engagement model

A predictable monthly rhythm

Compliance failures come from ad-hoc effort. We replace it with a fixed operating cycle.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic review

    We examine current books, past filings, open notices and registration status to establish a clean baseline.

  2. 02

    Calendar and responsibility map

    Every statutory due date for the year is scheduled with a named owner and an internal buffer.

  3. 03

    Monthly close

    Transactions recorded, bank and GST reconciled, ledgers reviewed and the month locked.

  4. 04

    Filing and assurance

    Returns filed, audit workpapers maintained and management informed of exposures before they escalate.

  5. 05

    Advisory review

    Quarterly discussion on tax posture, structure, cost control and readiness for lenders or buyers.
