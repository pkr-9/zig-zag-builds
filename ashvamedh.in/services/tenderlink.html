TenderLink — common questions

Direct answers about TenderLink and the way it fits within the Ashwamedh Group business ecosystem.

What is TenderLink?

TenderLink is Ashwamedh's tender and opportunity platform for discovering government, private and NGO opportunities, reviewing qualification requirements, preparing bids and tracking active pursuits.

Who is TenderLink designed for?

TenderLink is designed for MSMEs, contractors, established vendors and businesses entering formal tendering that need opportunity discovery, qualification and bid-support workflows.

Does TenderLink cover government and private tenders?

Yes. The service model covers government and PSU procurement as well as private buyer requirements and NGO or donor-funded programmes.

How does TenderLink help with tender qualification?

Opportunities can be reviewed against the bidder's registrations, turnover, past performance, certifications, capability and other stated eligibility requirements before significant bid effort is committed.

Does TenderLink help prepare the actual bid?

Yes. The service includes technical and financial response preparation, annexures, affidavits, formatting, submission checklists and clarification tracking.

Can TenderLink support a first-time bidder?

Yes. The service model includes registration guidance, bidder-profile preparation, document-vault setup, qualification review and structured support for the first submission.

Can tendering requirements be connected to other Ashwamedh services?

Yes. Tender requirements can connect with CA, legal, finance, hiring, civil and technology capabilities when the bid or resulting project requires multiple specialist functions.

How it works

From profile to award, in five deliberate steps

A structured cadence that keeps your team focused on winnable work rather than paperwork.

  1. 01

    Capability profiling

    We map your registrations, financials, manpower, certifications and past performance into a reusable bidder profile.

  2. 02

    Curated opportunity feed

    Matched tenders reach you with deadline, EMD, eligibility notes and a first-pass fit assessment attached.

  3. 03

    Bid or no-bid decision

    A short strategic review weighs competition, margin and delivery capacity before any effort is committed.

  4. 04

    Response assembly

    Technical write-ups, financial formats, statutory documents and digital signing handled to schedule.

  5. 05

    Submission and tracking

    Portal submission, clarification handling, opening updates and post-award documentation support.
