Civil & Interior — common questions

Direct answers about Civil & Interior and the way it fits within the Ashwamedh Group business ecosystem.

What does Civil & Interior cover?

Ashwamedh's Civil & Interior service covers civil construction, infrastructure and EPC work, commercial and residential interiors, design and drawings, procurement coordination and project management.

Who is the Civil & Interior service for?

The service is designed for infrastructure contractors, project-led organisations, commercial property owners and residential clients with construction or interior execution requirements.

Can Ashwamedh handle design and construction together?

Yes. The service model combines design, drawings, BOQ preparation, procurement coordination, construction or fit-out execution and project management.

What is the role of a BOQ in the Civil & Interior service?

The service uses a measured and priced bill of quantities to connect scope, quantities, specifications and project-cost discussions before execution.

Can you execute against an existing architect's drawings?

Yes. The existing service FAQ states that work can be executed against third-party drawings with coordination with the client's consultant during the project.

Does the service support commercial interiors?

Yes. The stated scope includes offices, showrooms, clinics, hospitality spaces and other commercial fit-outs with space planning, MEP coordination and finishing work.

Does Civil & Interior support project documentation and handover?

Yes. The service includes measured surveys, design documents, BOQs, schedules, progress reports, quality records, variation tracking and handover documentation.

Can civil projects connect with legal, finance or tender support?

Yes. The service can connect with legal documentation, project financing and tender participation where the wider project requirement crosses specialist functions.

Project documentation

What accompanies the physical work

A build is only well managed if it is well documented.

  • Measured site survey and feasibility note
  • Architectural layouts and 3D visualisation
  • Detailed BOQ with material specification
  • Project schedule with milestone dates
  • Weekly progress and photographic reports
  • Quality inspection and testing records
  • Variation register with cost impact
  • As-built drawings, warranties and handover pack
Execution stages

A construction sequence with no ambiguity

Each stage has a document, an approval and a payment milestone attached.

  1. 01

    Site study and brief

    Measurement, feasibility, statutory constraints and a written brief covering function, quality level and budget.

  2. 02

    Design and BOQ

    Layouts, visualisation and a detailed bill of quantities with specified materials and rates for approval.

  3. 03

    Approvals and mobilisation

    Statutory clearances, vendor finalisation, material scheduling and site setup ahead of work commencement.

  4. 04

    Execution and supervision

    Staged construction with quality checks, service coordination and weekly progress and photo reporting.

  5. 05

    Finishing and handover

    Snag resolution, final finishing, documentation, warranties and a completed handover pack.
