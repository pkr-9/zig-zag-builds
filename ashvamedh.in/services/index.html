Services Overview

What business services does Ashwamedh Group provide?

Ashwamedh Group provides an integrated business ecosystem covering tender intelligence, compliance, legal, technology, finance, civil and interior execution, and hiring services.

What does the TenderLink service cover?

Tender discovery, qualification and bid support.

What does the CA Service service cover?

Accounting, audit, taxation and corporate compliance.

What does the Legal Service service cover?

Contracts, commercial advisory, documentation and dispute support.

What does the IT Service service cover?

Software, cloud, cybersecurity, data and digital growth.
