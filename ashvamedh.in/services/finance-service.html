Finance Service — common questions

Direct answers about Finance Service and the way it fits within the Ashwamedh Group business ecosystem.

What does the Finance Service provide?

Ashwamedh's Finance Service supports working capital, project and term funding, bank guarantees, letters of credit, government scheme-linked funding, debt advisory and restructuring support.

Who is the Finance Service for?

The service is intended for MSMEs, contractors, project-led businesses and established organisations with working-capital, project-funding, guarantee or financial-structure requirements.

Can Ashwamedh help with working capital finance?

Yes. The service includes assessment and structuring of working-capital requirements around the business operating cycle and suitable banking or NBFC channels.

Can the Finance Service support project funding?

Yes. The service covers capital expenditure, plant and machinery and expansion-oriented project funding with a focus on matching the facility structure to the underlying requirement.

Can Ashwamedh arrange bank guarantees and letters of credit?

Yes. The stated scope includes performance and financial guarantees, EMD instruments and letters of credit for contract and trade obligations, subject to the applicable facility and sanction conditions.

Can Finance Service support a tender-related funding requirement?

Yes. Tender-related EMD, bank guarantees and working-capital requirements can be coordinated with TenderLink and other relevant Ashwamedh verticals.

Can Ashwamedh help with debt restructuring?

Yes. The service includes stress assessment, repayment rescheduling and lender-discussion support for businesses dealing with financing pressure.

Does Ashwamedh guarantee loan sanction?

No. The service explicitly states that the lender retains the credit decision. Ashwamedh's role is to assess the requirement, prepare the proposal and route it through appropriate funding channels.

Our role

Credit is rarely refused for the reason businesses assume

How funding happens

A disciplined path to sanction

Each stage removes a reason for the lender to say no.

  1. 01

    Requirement and eligibility

    We quantify the actual need, review financials and banking conduct, and assess realistic eligibility before anything is filed.

  2. 02

    Structuring

    The right mix of facility type, tenure, security and lender is selected against your cash flows.

  3. 03

    Proposal preparation

    CMA data, projections, promoter profile and supporting documentation assembled to underwriting standards.

  4. 04

    Lender engagement

    Submission, query resolution, credit discussion and negotiation on rate, margin and covenants.

  5. 05

    Sanction to disbursement

    Documentation, security creation, compliance conditions and drawdown, followed by ongoing monitoring.
