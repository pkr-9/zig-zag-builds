How TenderLink Works

From initial discovery to successful delivery, our process is designed to be simple, transparent, and highly effective.

1

Profile Setup

Create your business profile by adding details about your industry, turnover, past experience, and preferred locations. This helps our AI understand your capabilities.

Key Features

Select business categories (e.g., Construction, IT).
Upload certifications (MSME, ISO).
Set financial capabilities.
2

Smart Matching

Our algorithms scan thousands of new tenders daily. We filter out the noise and only present opportunities where you have a high probability of qualifying with our automated tender alerts.

Key Features

AI-driven eligibility checks.
Keyword-based filtering.
Exclusion of irrelevant tenders.
3

Detailed Analysis

Before you bid, access comprehensive reports. We provide competitor analysis, estimated project costs, and document checklists to ensure you are prepared for government procurement.

Key Features

Competitor rate benchmarking.
Document gap analysis.
Pre-bid query preparation.
4

Bid Preparation & Support

Our experts assist you in drafting the perfect bid. From technical write-ups to financial proposals, we ensure your submission is error-free and compliant via our end-to-end bid management.

Key Features

BoQ (Bill of Quantities) analysis.
Technical proposal drafting.
Final compliance review.
5

Submission & Tracking

Submit your bid with confidence. We help track the status of the tender from opening to evaluation and final award, keeping you updated at every stage of the bidding lifecycle.

Key Features

Submission deadline reminders.
Bid opening updates.
Award notification.

Need help setting up your profile?

Our procurement experts will configure your TenderLink profile to ensure you start receiving the perfect tender matches instantly.
