Why PUC Certificate is Mandatory?

The Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate is a mandatory document for all vehicles in India. It confirms that your vehicle's emissions are within the prescribed pollution norms.

  • Avoid Penalty: Fine up to ₹10,000 for non-compliance
  • Legal Requirement: Mandatory as per Central Motor Vehicle Rules
  • Environmental Protection: Reduces air pollution and protects health
  • Vehicle Health: Regular checks ensure optimal engine performance
  • Insurance Claims: Valid PUC required for insurance processing
  • Resale Value: Well-maintained vehicles fetch better prices
