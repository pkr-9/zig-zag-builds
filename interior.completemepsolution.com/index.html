Why it matters

Commercial interiors should be planned around building services

A commercial interior should not be planned only around furniture and finishes. HVAC airflow, AC placement, electrical routing, plumbing points, lighting, false ceiling levels, ventilation, access panels and maintenance routes should be considered early. This reduces site conflict and helps the workspace stay easier to operate.
